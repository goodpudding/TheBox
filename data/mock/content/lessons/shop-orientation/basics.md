# How we work in this shop

Shop layout, hours, sign-in, cleanup, and the rule: if you did not turn it on, do not turn it off. Staff will cover abandoned-property timing and a walkthrough of the floor.

## Dust and your lungs

- The hazard is **fine airborne wood dust**, not just the chips you can see on the floor.
- Use dust collection and respirators as posted for the machine and material.
- Fine dust can hang in the air after the shop looks clear — keep filtration/respirators in mind after cutting stops.
- Ask staff who empties collectors and where respirators live.
