# Printing on the Saturn

The Box runs an **Elegoo Saturn 2 (8K)** with Chitubox. Watch the assigned videos for the full workflow; this page is the shop framing.

## Shop rules that do not appear in YouTube

- Use the shop Chitubox profile for the resin we stock — do not invent exposure settings from a home printer.
- Check the FEP film before every job. Cloudy, scratched, or sagging film → stop and get staff.
- A failed print stuck to the vat film: **stop and get staff**. Digging at the FEP with a tool can puncture it and dump resin into the optics.
- Levelling and home-position checks are part of every session, not optional “once when new.”

## Unattended prints

Printers may run unattended. Heat/blade machines may not. Before walking away: lid closed, ventilation on, name and finish time on the machine.
