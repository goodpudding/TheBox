# Resin safety and PPE

This lesson is written for The Box. There is no outside video good enough to gate this certification on. Read it carefully — the quiz safety questions come from here.

## Why resin is different from FDM

Uncured photopolymer resin is a **skin sensitizer**. Repeated unprotected contact can produce a permanent allergy. Once that allergy exists, you may never be able to work with resin again. The hazard is chronic, not dramatic: nothing hurts the first time, and that is exactly how people get hurt.

IPA (isopropyl alcohol) used for washing is **flammable** and its vapour is an inhalation hazard. Treat both materials as chemical PPE jobs, not craft-station habits.

## Required PPE — every session

- **Nitrile gloves only** — never latex. Latex is permeable to resin chemistry.
- **Eye protection** — splash risk when pouring, scraping, or washing.
- **Long sleeves / apron** as needed so bare skin is not over the vat.
- **Ventilation running** before you open resin or IPA.

If any of those is missing, do not start.

## Skin contact

If uncured resin gets on bare skin:

1. Wash immediately with **soap and water**.
2. Do **not** wipe with IPA on skin — alcohol drives resin further in and strips the skin barrier.
3. Tell staff if exposure was more than a brief splash.

## Waste rules

- Cure **every** failed print, support, drip, and paper towel that touched liquid resin under UV before it goes in the trash.
- Liquid resin waste goes in the **labeled resin waste container** — not the sink, not the general trash.
- Used IPA that is too dirty to wash with goes in the **labeled used-IPA container**. Staff take it to King County Moderate Risk Waste. **Never pour IPA down the drain.**

## First session

Nobody runs resin unsupervised on their first session. Book a checkoff. The certification is the safety system for a machine members often leave running — treat the lesson accordingly.

## Before you walk away from a running print

Resin prints **may** run unattended (unlike the laser and plasma table). Before you leave:

- Gloves off and disposed or bagged correctly
- Lid closed
- Ventilation left running
- Your name and expected finish time on the machine
