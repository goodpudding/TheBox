# Washing and curing

Wash **then** cure. Curing a sticky, uncured surface locks contamination onto the part and leaves a contact hazard for the next person.

## Shop practice

- Use the wash/cure station as posted at the machine (model may differ from the Mercury XS in the video — the workflow is the same).
- Change IPA only per staff schedule; do not dump dirty wash liquid yourself except into the labeled used-IPA container.
- Nothing leaves the resin area uncured — including failed prints and supports.
- Cap IPA between uses. Keep it away from ignition sources (plasma table lives in the same building).
