# Sublimation overview

Sublimation turns printed dye into a gas under heat and bonds it into polyester (or polymer-coated blanks). Best-practice settings; confirm The Box's posted temperatures at the press.

## Hard rules

- Designs must be **mirrored** before printing (unless the software already mirrors for the product).
- Fabric garments need high polyester content — ideally **100% polyester** — and light colours. Cotton will not hold dye the same way.
- Heat press plates get hot enough to burn. Use heat-resistant tape, protect the press with butcher paper, and never leave a closed press unattended while it is cycling.
- Secure the transfer so it cannot shift — ghosting (double image) is almost always movement during open/close.
