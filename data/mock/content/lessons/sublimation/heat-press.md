# Heat press

Typical garment starting point (edit when The Box posts its chart): about **380–400°F**, firm pressure, ~45–60 seconds on 100% polyester — then peel carefully while hot surfaces stay hot.

## Every press

1. Lint-roll and pre-press briefly to remove moisture/wrinkles.
2. Butcher paper (or press pillow + paper) so ink cannot contaminate the platen or the back of the shirt.
3. Tape the transfer; do not rely on gravity.
4. Stay with the press for the full cycle.
5. Cool on a clean surface; do not fold a hot transfer against itself.
