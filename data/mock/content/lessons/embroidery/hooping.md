# Hooping and stabilizer

Most embroidery failures are hooping or stabilizer failures, not machine failures.

## Stabilizer cheat sheet

- **Cutaway** — knits, t-shirts, anything that stretches. It stays behind the design for the life of the garment.
- **Tearaway** — stable wovens (canvas, denim, many towels) where you want the backing gone after stitching.
- **Water-soluble topper** — towels, fleece, and pile fabrics so stitches sit on top instead of sinking in.

## Hooping

Layer outer hoop → stabilizer → fabric → inner hoop. Fabric should be taut like a drum **without** stretching the grain. Excess fabric tucked so it cannot catch the embroidery arm.

If the design puckers after unhooping, you probably stretched the fabric in the hoop — re-hoop; do not pull harder mid-stitch.
