# Embroidery machine basics

Best-practice path for The Box's embroidery station (Brother-class single-needle; confirm the exact model at the machine). Hands-on checkoff is still required.

## Core habits

- Needle up, presser foot rules, and bobbin orientation follow the machine's numbered path — same discipline as sewing.
- **Never** pull fabric while the machine is stitching; the hoop and feed must control movement.
- Keep fingers clear of the needle and the moving hoop arm.
- Stop immediately on a bird's nest, broken needle, or unusual noise — find every needle fragment before restarting.

## Files

Bring designs in the shop's accepted formats (typically PES / DST — confirm at the station). USB workflow is covered in the PE800 beginner video.
