# Shop Steward

Volunteer **four hours a week** (one shift) and receive a **free Maker membership** plus free certifications — modeled on Seattle Makers’ Maketeer program.

Stewards cover open hours so a small shop can run evenings and weekends with two full-time staff. You also get permission to **schedule maintenance blocks** on any machine when something needs cleaning or repair.

Want to go deeper on a specific tool? Look at **Tool Champion** — a two-month maintain-and-train commitment that can lead to the Shop lead track.

Apply below (choose Shop Steward).
